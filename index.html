Our idea

From “I hope it's good” to “I know more about it.”

Trust Your Food is a concept for connecting restaurant menus with clear, accessible sourcing information.

01 / Restaurant

Share ingredient details

Restaurants provide supplier names, sourcing locations, and relevant records for selected ingredients.

02 / Customer

Scan and explore

A simple QR-linked page helps diners see the information behind a dish.

03 / Transparency

Build informed trust

Clear sourcing disclosures make conversations about food quality more concrete.
